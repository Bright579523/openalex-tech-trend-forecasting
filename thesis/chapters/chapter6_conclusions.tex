\section{Limitations}
In accordance with transparent academic practice, this section explicitly delineates study boundaries:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Bibliographic Proxy Limitations:} Publication volume measures scholarly output rather than patent commercialization, industrial adoption, or technology readiness levels.
    \item \textbf{Retrospective Classifier Dynamics:} OpenAlex topic clusters and classifier assignments (Qwen3-8B deployed October 2026) are applied retrospectively across the 2010--2024 period, reflecting current thematic boundaries rather than contemporaneous classification.
    \item \textbf{National Scope:} Findings reflect the research ecosystems of Germany, China, and Japan and may not generalize uniformly to smaller, emerging, or less centralized science systems.
\end{enumerate}

Outlines future research opportunities, including multi-modal data integration (patents, clinical trials, funding grants), non-linear machine learning architectures, and broader cross-country comparative panels.
