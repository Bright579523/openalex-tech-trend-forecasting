\section{RQ1: Longitudinal Evolution of National Specialisation (2010--2024)}
\emph{[To be populated upon completion of Stage 3 Feature Engineering and Descriptive Analysis.]}
This section presents the empirical trajectory of technology topics across Germany, China, and Japan over the 15-year study period. It analyzes the distribution of Fractional All-Science Revealed Comparative Advantage ($RCA_{\text{AllSci}}^{\text{Frac}}$) across the five technology fields and identifies key thematic concentrations.

\section{RQ2: Forecast Accuracy Analysis}
\emph{[To be populated upon execution of Stage 3 Ridge Regression modeling.]}
This section evaluates whether incorporating national publication volumes ($M2$) and national specialization metrics ($M3$) reduces Mean Absolute Error (MAE) relative to naive adjusted momentum ($M0$) and global history ($M1$) across Test Origins 2018 and 2021. Bootstrap confidence intervals quantify the statistical significance of predictive gains.

\section{RQ3: Early Identification of Emerging High-Growth Topics}
\emph{[To be populated upon evaluation of the Precision@50 protocol.]}
This section examines model efficacy in identifying nascent topics that subsequently become high-growth among candidates outside the historical top growth quintile. Performance is benchmarked against the 20\% chance baseline and the naive momentum ranker.

\section{Discussion and Policy Implications}
This section synthesizes the empirical findings, interpreting the degree to which national scientific specialization serves as a leading indicator of global technological trends.
